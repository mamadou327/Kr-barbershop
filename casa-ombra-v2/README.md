# Casa Ombra v2: plan-first rebuild

Same style as v1 (warm board-marked concrete, travertine, timber lattice, black pool,
olive trees, M3 CS + Mansory Urus outside, SVJ + SF90 in a glass garage), but a simple
house that is easy to keep consistent across every image.

## The house
- Ground floor: one rectangle 36 x 12 m. Glass along the pool side. A single-storey
  strip along the back (garage, WC, coats, cinema, pantry, utility, gym).
- First floor: one long concrete box 36 x 10 m with a 3 m overhang on the pool side
  (the covered terrace). Timber lattice on the pool side and the west end.
- Site: forecourt at the west end, pool 20 x 4 m parallel to the house, lawn with
  three olive trees, road along the south.

## Steps
1. Brief: done (this file)
2. Plans: `plan/1-ground-floor.png`, `plan/2-first-floor.png`
3. Bird's-eye 3D from the plans: locks the shape
4. Hero exterior shot (plan + bird's-eye as references, camera spot named)
5. Reference pack = plan + bird's-eye + hero, attached to every prompt after this
6. Other exteriors: night, aerial (drone), forecourt, garage
7. Furnished 3D floor plans
8. Interiors, one at a time, each checked against the plan
9. Videos (Seedance) from approved stills
10. Website + content

Use only unlimited models (Nano Banana Pro for images).
