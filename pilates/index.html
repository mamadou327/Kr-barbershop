Classes

Three classes. Pick your pace.

Every class runs on the reformer with a teacher in the room. New to it? Start with Foundations and we'll show you the springs, the straps and the footbar.

50 min · New to reformer

Foundations

Slower and clear. You learn the set-up, breathing and the core moves, so you can join any class after four sessions.

Typical springs: 1 red, 1 blue
50 min · All levels

Flow

One move into the next with few breaks. Long lines, steady breath and a good stretch at the end.

Typical springs: 1 red, 1 yellow
45 min · Some experience

Sculpt

Heavier springs, slower reps and more holds. Legs, glutes and arms, with your core working the whole time.

Typical springs: 2 red, 1 blue
