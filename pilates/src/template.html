The studio

One room off Bermondsey Street with eight reformers, two arched windows and an olive tree. Classes run fifty minutes, the springs are set before you arrive, and nobody has to fight for a spot.

Classes

Four ways onto the carriage.

  • FoundationsSlower and clear. The set-up, the straps and the breath, so you can join any class after four sessions.50 min, new to reformer
  • FlowOne move into the next with few breaks. Long lines, steady breath and a proper stretch at the end.50 min, all levels
  • SculptHeavier springs, slower reps, longer holds. Legs, glutes and arms, with your core on the whole time.45 min, some experience
  • PrivateOne teacher, one reformer, the whole room. Rehab, pregnancy or a programme built around a goal.55 min, by request
