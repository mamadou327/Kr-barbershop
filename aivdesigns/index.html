More than a website. A reason to choose you

We build for kitchen studios, estate agents, gyms, salons and trades: businesses that need to look like the best choice in town, then get the customer to book.

AIV Designs is a UK web design studio built on one belief: customers decide who to trust in the first few seconds. So we don't just build pages. We build first impressions, backed by a first-class user experience.

You deal directly with the people designing your site, and you see your homepage built before you pay a penny. Our aim is simple: give local businesses the kind of website usually kept for big brands, and make every one earn its place.

  • Film firstYour place and your work, moving on screen before a customer reads a word.
  • See it before you payA free concept of your homepage within 48 hours. Nothing to pay until you approve it.
  • Built to one goalEvery page leads to the one action you need: a booking, a viewing or a quote.

Four steps, then you're live

  1. 01

    Tell us about your business

    A short call or a quick form: what you sell, who your customers are and what the site needs them to do. Send photos if you have them, or we can photograph your place.

  2. 02

    Free concept

    We design your homepage and send it to you moving, on a private link, with a fixed price for the full site. Nothing to pay until you approve it.

  3. 03

    Build and review

    We build the full site: every page, the words, the film, the forms and the phone version. You try it on your own phone and send your changes in one list.

  4. 04

    Launch and look after

    We connect your domain, set up Google and go live. Then we host it, keep it secure and update it whenever you need.

Step 1 of 4

Before you ask

  1. 01 / 08

    How much does a website cost?

    Every site is priced for the job: how many pages it needs, how much motion and film, and whether we photograph your place or create the scenes from your photos. You get a fixed quote with your free concept, before you pay anything, and you can spread the cost monthly if that suits you better.

  2. 02 / 08

    How long does it take?

    It depends on how big the site is and whether we photograph your place. We agree the timings with you before we start, and they're written into your quote.

  3. 03 / 08

    What do I need to give you?

    Your business name, what you offer and a few photos of your place or your work. If you don't have good photos, we can photograph your place for you, which adds a little to the price, or create the scenes from what you have. We write the words with you.

  4. 04 / 08

    What if I don't like the free concept?

    Then you walk away and pay nothing. The concept is free, with no obligation. If it's close but not quite right, tell us what to change and we'll update it.

  5. 05 / 08

    Can I change things after it's live?

    Yes. Send us new prices, photos or opening hours and we update them for you. Bigger changes, like a new page, get a fixed quote first.

  6. 06 / 08

    How do customers find it on Google?

    We set up your Google Business profile, page titles and descriptions, and submit the site to Google at launch.

  7. 07 / 08

    Do I own the website?

    Yes. The domain is registered in your name and you get every file. If you ever leave, we hand everything over.

  8. 08 / 08

    What if I already have a website?

    We can rebuild it or add a moving homepage on top of what you have. We keep your domain and your email working.

Privacy policy

Last updated 18 November 2026

Who we are

AIV Designs is a web design studio run as a sole trader business in the UK. We are responsible for the personal information described here. You can reach us about anything in this policy at hello@aivdesigns.co.uk.

What we collect

  • When you enquire. Your name, business name, email or phone number, type of business and anything you write in your message.
  • When you become a client. What we need to do the work and get paid: billing details, the photos, words and files you send us, and any logins you share so we can set up your domain or Google profile.
  • When you visit this site. We may use privacy-friendly analytics that count visits and see which pages are read, without cookies and without identifying you.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and make your free concept, because you asked us to.
  • To build, host and look after your website and to invoice you, because that is our agreement with you.
  • To keep financial records, because tax law requires it.
  • To understand how this site is used and improve it, which is in our legitimate interest and has very little effect on you.

We never sell your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists. We only email you about your enquiry or your project. If we ever want to send you news, we will ask first.

Who we share it with

Only the services that help us run the business, such as our email provider, website hosting, payment provider, analytics and accounting software. They may only use your information to provide their service to us. Some of them store data outside the UK. When they do, it is protected by UK-approved safeguards.

How long we keep it

If your enquiry does not go ahead, we delete your details within 12 months. If you become a client, we keep your records for as long as we work together and then for as long as tax law requires, currently about six years.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, and ask us to correct it, delete it, or stop or limit how we use it. Email hello@aivdesigns.co.uk and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Cookies

This website does not use cookies for tracking or advertising.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Terms of business

Last updated 18 November 2026

1. About these terms

These terms apply when AIV Designs designs, builds, hosts or looks after a website for you. Your quote sets out the work, the price and the timings. Together with these terms, it forms our agreement. If your quote says something different from these terms, your quote applies.

2. Your free concept

The free concept costs nothing and you are under no obligation to go ahead. Until you do, the concept and the files we made for it stay ours.

3. Quotes and prices

Every quote is a fixed price for the work it describes and is valid for 30 days. If you want something that is not in your quote, we will tell you the price before we do it. We are not VAT registered, so no VAT is added to our prices. If that changes, we will tell you before it affects anything you pay.

4. Paying us

  • Paying for the build. Half when you approve the concept and we start, half before your site goes live.
  • Paying monthly. Monthly payments are taken by Direct Debit. Your quote sets out the monthly amount and the minimum term.
  • Invoices are due within 14 days. If a payment is late, we will remind you first. If it is still unpaid, we may pause work or hosting until it is paid, and we may charge interest as the law allows for late payment between businesses.

5. What we need from you

We need your words, photos, feedback and access to things like your domain on time. If we are waiting on you, the timings in your quote move to match. If a project is on hold for more than 60 days because we have not heard from you, we may close it and invoice for the work done so far.

6. Reviews and changes

Your build includes two rounds of changes. You send each round as one list. Further rounds, new pages or new features are quoted before we start them.

7. Photography and generated images

If we photograph your place, the cost is included in your quote. You confirm we are allowed to take photos there, and that anyone who appears in them has agreed. We may also create scenes with image tools, using your own photos as a guide. You approve every image and film before your site goes live.

8. Who owns the website

Once you have paid in full, the design, words, images and film we made for your website are yours. If you pay monthly, they become yours when the minimum term has been paid. Your domain is registered in your name. Some parts, like fonts, code libraries and stock images, are licensed from others and come with their own terms. We keep the right to reuse our general methods and code, but never your content.

9. Hosting and looking after your site

If we host and look after your site, your quote says what is included, such as hosting, security updates and small changes. After any minimum term, either of us can end this with 30 days' notice. When it ends, we hand over your files and help you move your site.

10. Showing your website

We may show your website in our portfolio and on social media. If you would rather we did not, tell us and we will not.

11. Your content

You confirm that you have the right to use everything you send us, such as your logo, photos and words. You are responsible for the facts on your site, such as your prices, opening hours and any claims about your business.

12. Our responsibility

We do our work with reasonable care and skill. We cannot promise a particular Google ranking or number of sales. Our total liability to you is limited to what you paid us in the 12 months before the claim, and we are not liable for indirect losses or lost profits. Nothing in these terms limits liability that the law does not allow us to limit.

13. Ending the agreement

You can end the project at any time before your site goes live. You pay for the work done up to that point, and the deposit covers the early stages. We can end the agreement if you break these terms and do not put it right within 14 days of us asking.

14. The law that applies

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, and any dispute will be dealt with by its courts.

15. Contact

Questions about these terms? Email hello@aivdesigns.co.uk.
